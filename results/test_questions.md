# Evaluation Test Questions

Instructions: Ask each question in the chatbot app, then fill in the
"Actual Answer" and "Status" columns based on what it says.
Status options: Correct / Partially Correct / Incorrect / Refused (out of scope)

## Category A: Direct questions that match document wording closely

1. What are the admission requirements for undergraduate programs?
2. When is the application deadline?
3. What documents are required for application?
4. What is the tuition fee for the BIT program?
5. Are scholarships available?
6. What courses are offered in the BIT program?
7. Is there a final year project?
8. Are internships part of the curriculum?
9. Can students switch programs after enrollment?
10. How many credit hours are required to graduate?
11. What is the minimum CGPA required to remain in good standing?
12. Can students defer a semester?
13. What is the policy on late assignment submissions?
14. What is the policy on academic dishonesty?
15. What are the library operating hours?
16. Is on-campus accommodation available?
17. Where can students seek counseling support?
18. What sports facilities are there?
19. How can students contact the Academic Office?
20. Who do students contact for IT support?
21. Who should students contact regarding internship placements?

## Category B: Reworded or casual phrasing to test semantic retrieval

22. Can I get a scholarship?
23. What happens if I submit my assignment late?
24. Is there mental health support on campus?

## Category C: Out-of-scope questions that should be refused gracefully instead of hallucinated

25. What's the weather like today?
26. What is the tuition fee for the Medicine program?
27. Who is the Prime Minister of Malaysia?

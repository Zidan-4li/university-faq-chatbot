UNIVERSITY STUDENT HANDBOOK - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SECTION 1: ADMISSIONS

Q: What are the admission requirements for undergraduate programs?
A: Applicants must have completed secondary education with a minimum CGPA of 2.5,
or equivalent qualification. English proficiency is required, with a minimum
IELTS score of 6.0 or TOEFL score of 550 for international students.

Q: When is the application deadline?
A: Applications for the September intake close on 31 July. Applications for the
February intake close on 31 December.

Q: What documents are required for application?
A: Applicants must submit certified academic transcripts, a copy of their
identification document or passport, two passport-sized photographs, and proof
of English proficiency (if applicable).

SECTION 2: FEES AND FINANCIAL AID

Q: What is the tuition fee for the Bachelor of Information Technology program?
A: The tuition fee for the BIT program is RM 45,000 for the full duration of
study, payable in installments per semester.

Q: Are scholarships available?
A: Yes, the university offers Merit Scholarships (up to 50% tuition waiver for
students with CGPA above 3.5) and Need-Based Financial Aid for eligible students.
Applications must be submitted before the start of each academic year.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Tuition fees can be paid via bank transfer, online payment portal, or
in-person at the finance office. Payment plans can be arranged with the
Bursary Office.

SECTION 3: COURSES AND ACADEMIC POLICIES

Q: How many credit hours are required to graduate with a BIT degree?
A: Students must complete a minimum of 120 credit hours, including core
courses, electives, and a final year project, to graduate.

Q: What is the minimum CGPA required to remain in good academic standing?
A: Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.0. Students falling below this
threshold will be placed on academic probation.

Q: Can students defer a semester?
A: Yes, students may apply for deferment for a maximum of two consecutive
semesters. A formal request must be submitted to the Academic Office at
least four weeks before the semester begins.

Q: What is the policy on late assignment submissions?
A: Late submissions are penalized at 10% of the total marks per day, up to a
maximum of 3 days late. Submissions later than 3 days will not be accepted
unless supported by a valid medical certificate or documented emergency.

SECTION 4: CAMPUS FACILITIES AND SERVICES

Q: What are the library operating hours?
A: The main library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and 9:00 AM
to 6:00 PM on weekends.

Q: Is on-campus accommodation available?
A: Yes, the university provides on-campus dormitories with single and shared
room options. Applications for accommodation should be submitted at least one
month before the semester starts.

Q: Where can students seek counseling or mental health support?
A: The Student Wellness Center, located in Block C, offers free counseling
services for enrolled students. Appointments can be booked through the student
portal.

SECTION 5: CONTACT INFORMATION

Q: How can students contact the Academic Office?
A: The Academic Office can be reached via email at academic@university.edu.my
or by phone at 03-1234-5678, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Q: Who do students contact for IT support?
